PUT SHOT TRACKER ON YOUR PHONE
==============================

This puts the app on a server on the internet, so your phone can use it
anywhere without your computer. Same app, same accuracy. It gets its own
icon on your Home Screen and opens full screen, like any other app.

Setting it up takes about 15 minutes, once, on your Mac.


WHAT IT COSTS
-------------
The server is at Fly.io, which charges only while it is running. It goes
to sleep a few minutes after you stop using it and wakes up in a few
seconds when you open it, so a few sessions a week should come to a few
dollars a month. Current prices: fly.io/docs/about/pricing. Fly asks for a
credit card when you sign up.


YOUR CLIPS
----------
- Nobody gets in without the password.
- Each clip is deleted from the server 2 hours after it is analysed, and
  every clip is deleted whenever the server goes to sleep. The speeds,
  marks and history are kept.
- A clip you have just uploaded plays from your phone, not the server.
- "Save video with shots" needs the server's copy, so save it right after
  the session, while the clip is still there.


1. INSTALL FLY'S TOOL (ONCE)
----------------------------
Open Terminal (Applications > Utilities > Terminal) and paste this line,
then press Return:

    curl -L https://fly.io/install.sh | sh

When it finishes, paste this line and press Return:

    echo 'export PATH="$HOME/.fly/bin:$PATH"' >> ~/.zshrc && source ~/.zshrc

(If you use Homebrew, "brew install flyctl" does the same.)


2. MAKE A FLY ACCOUNT (ONCE)
----------------------------

    fly auth signup

A web page opens: sign up there. Then, on fly.io, add a credit card under
Billing. (Already have an account? Use "fly auth login" instead.)


3. PUT THE APP ON IT (ONCE)
---------------------------
Unzip Hockey-Shot-Tracker.zip. In Terminal, type  cd  and a space, drag the
Hockey-Shot-Tracker folder onto the Terminal window, and press Return.
Then:

    bash deploy/fly-setup.sh

It asks for:
- a name for your app, which becomes the address: NAME.fly.dev
- the region nearest you (it shows the list)
- a password: the one you'll type on your phone, 8 characters or more

Then it builds and starts the app, which takes a few minutes the first
time. At the end it prints your address.

If it stops with an error, copy everything it printed and send it to me.


4. ON YOUR PHONE
----------------
- Open https://NAME.fly.dev in Safari and type the password.
- Tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen".
- From then on, open it from its icon. You stay logged in.

If it has been asleep, the first open takes a few seconds.


USING IT
--------
Just as before:
- Tap "Choose clips" and pick your videos from Photos.
- Fill in the form. For your backyard that's 18.75 ft, lens 0.5x, phone
  on the ground.
- Tap Analyze.

Big 4K clips upload slowly over cellular; on Wi-Fi they are quick. 1080p at
60 fps is plenty (Settings > Camera > Record Video).


WHEN I SEND A NEW VERSION
-------------------------
Unzip it, go into the new folder in Terminal (the cd step in 3), and run:

    bash deploy/fly-update.sh

Your sessions and your password stay as they were.


CHANGING THINGS LATER
---------------------
- A new password (every phone has to log in again):
      fly secrets set SHOTTRACKER_PASSWORD=your-new-password
- Keep clips for longer or shorter: change SHOTTRACKER_KEEP_CLIPS_HOURS in
  fly.toml, then run bash deploy/fly-update.sh.
- Take it down for good (deletes everything on the server):
      fly apps destroy NAME

The home version ("Start Hockey App") still works exactly as before.

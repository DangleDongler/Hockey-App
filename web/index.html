Mark the net

Mark the goal itself — the posts and crossbar a puck goes between. If your net sits inside a larger backstop frame, do not mark the backstop: it is wider than the goal, and marking it makes every speed read low by the same proportion.

  1. Click near the top-left of the goal frame
  2. Then the top-right
  3. Then the bottom-right, where the post meets the ground
  4. Then the bottom-left
  5. Drag the corners until the dashed lines sit on the outside of the pipe

A magnifier follows your cursor — use it to land on the pipe itself. Each edge is extended across the frame as a dashed line: line those up with the straight lengths of pipe rather than aiming at the corners. Real goal corners are bends, so the corner is where the two straight sections would meet. Once all four are down, drag any corner to nudge it; the green outline previews the opening.
